Privacy Policy

Video Download Service — Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. What We Collect

When you place an order we collect only what is needed to fulfill it:

  • Your email address (to deliver the download link);
  • The video URL you submit and your subtitle options;
  • Order details: amount, payment status, language preference, and timestamps.

Payments are processed by Paddle as merchant of record. We never receive or store your card number or other full payment credentials.

2. How We Use It

  • To fulfill your order and email you the download link;
  • To respond to support, refund, and replacement requests;
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of the service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Cookies and Analytics

The order page does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Your browser's local storage may keep your language preference so the page displays in your language on return visits.

4. Where Your Data Lives

Order data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. Emails are sent through our email delivery provider. These providers process data on our behalf solely to operate the service.

5. Retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfill the order, handle refunds or replacements, and meet legal and accounting obligations. You may request earlier deletion (see below).

6. Sharing

We share data only with the processors named above (Paddle, Cloudflare, our email provider) and where required by law. Nothing else.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing support@echoshorts.win from the address used in your order.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: support@echoshorts.win
